# HELPER × Devin — 90-Second Demo Script

**0:00–0:12 — Mission**

“HELPER is a nonprofit platform for direct human-to-human help. The platform takes 0% of aid intended for recipients, and the operator is designed not to hold aid-moving authority.”

**0:12–0:28 — Discover**

“People can discover bounded needs such as food, rides, or labor. The demo data is illustrative; the point is the product loop.”

**0:28–0:47 — Trust**

“We refuse to collapse trust into one opaque score. Need Confidence, Fraud Risk, and Recipient Authority are separate. The Need Confidence Graph stays in shadow mode and cannot control aid.”

**0:47–1:02 — Direct route**

“The recipient authorizes the destination and route terms. HELPER cannot silently replace that route, and the platform fee is zero. This demo does not execute real money; mainnet remains disabled.”

**1:02–1:17 — Proof lifecycle**

“We also refuse to call funding ‘success.’ Broadcast, finality, delivery, evidence, proof, disputes, appeals, and resolution stay separate so the UI cannot outrun reality.”

**1:17–1:30 — Why Devin**

“This is where Devin + Astra fits: repository hardening, invariant tests, trust-flow integration, browser E2E, security/edge-case work, and video evidence. We want Devin as an engineering teammate under explicit constraints — never as an authority over aid.”
